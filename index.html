Construction OCR / AI Cashflow

OCR scanning of receipts & invoices → automated cashflow

100 receipts a week collected in a folder and handed to the accountant manually. Some got lost. Bank reconciliation took 2 hours — and was still inaccurate.
400
receipts/month auto-processed
0
lost documents

What was done

Mobile capture — foreman photographs a receipt or invoice and sends it to the Telegram bot. No folders, no physical storage
OCR + AI parsing — OpenAI Vision extracts amount, date, counterparty and expense category. Python script validates and normalizes data
Auto-write to cashflow — Make passes structured data to the spreadsheet. Expense appears in cashflow instantly, no accountant needed
Bank reconciliation — spreadsheet contains all expenses with dates and amounts, reconciliation takes minutes instead of 2 hours
The accountant gets a ready cashflow in real time — no folders, no losses, no manual entry. 400 documents per month processed automatically.

Insight: losing 5% of 400 receipts means blind spots in finances worth tens of thousands. Automation eliminates the human factor entirely.
